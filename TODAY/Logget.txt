Yes, exactly! That is the core power of this monitoring stack.

When you make routing changes (like OSPF or BGP state changes) or modify VLANs, your routers and switches instantly generate syslog messages. Those messages get sent straight to **Grafana Alloy**, stored in **Loki**, and you can view them live in **Grafana** with precise timestamps.

This means you can instantly see things like:

* **OSPF neighbor state changes** (e.g., going from `FULL` to `DOWN` and back up).
* **BGP prefix updates or session resets.**
* **Interface status updates** when you modify a VLAN or shut down a port.
* **Exact timestamps** for when a network event happened so you can correlate configuration changes with downtime or connectivity issues.
